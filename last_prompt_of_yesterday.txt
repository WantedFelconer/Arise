In Flutter, a Positioned widget must be a direct child of a Stack. Because RepaintBoundary sat between Stack and Positioned, Flutter threw Incorrect use of ParentDataWidget on every animation tick and whenever the virtual keyboard (IME) opened or closed.

2. Why Connection timed out. The system core took too long to respond Occurred
This error is thrown by the Flutter client when it cannot reach the backend within 10 seconds. On Android, this typically happens due to one of the following:

A. Missing Android Cleartext HTTP Permission
Android 9+ (API 28+) blocks all unencrypted http:// traffic by default unless explicitly allowed. Without this, Android silently drops all connections to http://10.0.2.2:3000 or http://localhost:3000, causing the request to time out.

Fix Applied: Added android:usesCleartextTraffic="true" and <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET"/> to
